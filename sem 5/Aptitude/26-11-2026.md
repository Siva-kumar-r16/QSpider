# Aptitude – Day 4

## Remainders in LCM & HCF

### LCM

- **Single remainder** = first find LCM & add the remainder
- **Multiple remainders** = first find LCM & subtract the remainder

> ⚠️ **Check:** for multiple remainders, subtract the **common difference** (divisor − remainder). This works when that difference is the same for every divisor.

### HCF

- **Single remainder** = first subtract & then find HCF
- **Multiple remainder** = first subtract & then find HCF

### Find the least number which is divisible by 12, 16, 18, 20 least remainder 21

```text
2 | 12, 16, 18, 20
2 |  6,  8,  9, 10
2 |  3,  4,  9,  5
2 |  3,  2,  9,  5
3 |  3,  1,  9,  5
3 |  1,  1,  3,  5
5
```

2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 3 × 3 × 5 = **720**

Remainder = 21

720 + 21 ⇒ **741**

> ⚠️ **Check:** a remainder cannot be larger than its divisor, and 21 is larger than 12, 16, 18 and 20. 741 follows the class method, but it does not leave remainder 21 on division. Please confirm the question.

### Least 4-digit number

741 × 2 = 1440 ⇒ 1440 + 21 = **1461**

> ⚠️ **Check:** the first step should read **720 × 2** = 1440. The same problem with the remainder 21 applies here.

### 4 bells ring at 10, 12, 15, 40 seconds. The bells ring at 8:30 am. When will they ring the next time all together?

```text
5 | 10, 12, 15, 40
2 |  2, 12,  3,  4
2 |  1,  6,  3,  2
3 |  1,  3,  3,  1
```

LCM = 5 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 3 = **120**

8:30 am ⇒ **8:32 am**

> ⚠️ **Check:** the answer is right, but 40 ÷ 5 is **8**, not 4. The corrected division is:
>
> ```text
> 5 | 10, 12, 15, 40
> 2 |  2, 12,  3,  8
> 2 |  1,  6,  3,  4
> 3 |  1,  3,  3,  2
> 2 |  1,  1,  1,  2
>       1,  1,  1,  1
> ```

### HCF & LCM of 2 no. is 8 & 96. Sum of those no. is 56

8 × 96 = 56 × x

x = (8 × 96) / 56 = **13.714**

> ⚠️ **Check:** 56 is the **sum**, not one of the numbers.
> - Let the numbers be 8a and 8b, where a and b have no common factor.
> - 8 × a × b = 96, so a × b = 12.
> - 8a + 8b = 56, so a + b = 7.
> - a = 3, b = 4, so the numbers are **24 and 32**.
