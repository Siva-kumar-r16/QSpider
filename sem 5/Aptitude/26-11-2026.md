# Aptitude – Day 4

## Remainders in LCM & HCF

### LCM

* **Single remainder:** First find $\text{LCM}$ and add the remainder.
* **Multiple remainders:** First find $\text{LCM}$ and subtract the common difference ($\text{divisor} - \text{remainder}$).

### HCF

* **Single remainder:** First subtract the remainder from each number, then find $\text{HCF}$.
* **Multiple remainders:** First subtract each remainder from its corresponding number, then find $\text{HCF}$.

---

### 1. Find the least number which is divisible by $12, 16, 18, 20$ and leaves remainder $21$

```text
2 | 12, 16, 18, 20
2 |  6,  8,  9, 10
2 |  3,  4,  9,  5
2 |  3,  2,  9,  5
3 |  3,  1,  9,  5
3 |  1,  1,  3,  5
5 |  1,  1,  1,  5
  |  1,  1,  1,  1
```

$$
\text{LCM} = 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 3 \times 5 = \mathbf{720}
$$

$$\text{Remainder} = 21$$

$$
720 + 21 = \mathbf{741}
$$

#### Least 4-digit number

$$
720 \times 2 = 1440 \implies 1440 + 21 = \mathbf{1461}
$$

*(Note: Strictly speaking, a remainder must be smaller than every divisor, so $21$ is only valid if the question meant "decreased by $21$ is divisible by $12, 16, 18, 20$".)*

### 2. Find the least number divided by $5, 8, 19$ & leaves remainder $2, 5, 16$

```text
 2 | 5, 8, 19
 2 | 5, 4, 19
 2 | 5, 2, 19
 5 | 5, 1, 19
19 | 1, 1, 19
   | 1, 1,  1
```

$$
\text{LCM} = 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 5 \times 19 = \mathbf{760}
$$

Common difference:

```text
  5    8    19
- 2  - 5  - 16
--------------
  3    3     3
```

$$
760 - 3 = \mathbf{757}
$$

---

### 3. Find the greatest no. which when dividing $148, 52$ leaves a remainder $4$

$$
148 - 4 = 144, \quad 52 - 4 = 48
$$

```text
2 | 144, 48
2 |  72, 24
2 |  36, 12
2 |  18,  6
3 |   9,  3
  |   3,  1
```

$$
\text{HCF} = 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 3 = \mathbf{48}
$$

### 4. What greatest number divides $17, 42$ & $93$ & leaves remainder $2, 13, 15$

*(Note: With remainders $2, 13, 15$, the differences are $15, 29, 78$, giving $\text{HCF} = \mathbf{1}$. Using the corrected working $13, 39, 78$ from the notes, which corresponds to remainders $4, 3, 15$:)*

### 5. $4$ bells ring at $10, 12, 15, 40$ seconds. The bells ring at $8:30\text{ am}$. When will they ring the next time all together?

```text
5 | 10, 12, 15, 40
2 |  2, 12,  3,  8
2 |  1,  6,  3,  4
3 |  1,  3,  3,  2
2 |  1,  1,  1,  2
  |  1,  1,  1,  1
```

$$
\text{LCM} = 5 \times 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 2 = \mathbf{120\text{ seconds}} = 2\text{ minutes}
$$

$$
8:30\text{ am} + 2\text{ minutes} = \mathbf{8:32\text{ am}}
$$

### 6. $\text{HCF}$ & $\text{LCM}$ of $2$ no. is $8$ & $96$. Sum of those no. is $56$

Let the two numbers be $8a$ and $8b$ (where $a$ and $b$ are coprime):

* $8ab = 96 \implies ab = 12$
* $8a + 8b = 56 \implies a + b = 7$

Solving gives $a = 3$ and $b = 4$:

$$
8 \times 3 = \mathbf{24}, \quad 8 \times 4 = \mathbf{32}
$$
