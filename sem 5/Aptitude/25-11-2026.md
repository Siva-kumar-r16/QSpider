## Product of Two Numbers

**Product of two numbers = HCF × LCM**

(a × b) = HCF × LCM

### 1. LCM of 2 numbers = 40, HCF of 2 numbers = 3, one of the numbers = 60. Find the other no.

(40 × 3) / 60 = **2**

> ⚠️ **Check:** the arithmetic is right, but no such pair of numbers exists, because the HCF (3) must divide the LCM (40). Please confirm the question's values.
