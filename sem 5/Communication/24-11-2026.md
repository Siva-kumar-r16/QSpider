## Sentence Structure

### 4 Types of Questions

1. Sentence Formation
2. Sentence Correction
3. Sentence Arrangement
4. Paragraph Formation

> ➕ **Added**
> - **Sentence Formation:** build a correct sentence from given words. *go / I / daily / college / to* → I go to college daily.
> - **Sentence Correction:** find and fix the error. *She go to college.* → She goes to college.
> - **Sentence Arrangement:** put jumbled parts in order. *in the lab / the students / are working* → The students are working in the lab.
> - **Paragraph Formation:** arrange sentences into a logical paragraph: opening idea, supporting points, closing.

## Exception for S + V + O

> ➕ **Added:** S = Subject, V = Verb, O = Object, C = Complement. The usual order is S + V + O: *I (S) write (V) code (O).*

### 1. S + V

No object needed

> ➕ **Added:** *The baby sleeps.*

### 2. S + V + C

Linking Verbs

> ➕ **Added:** a linking verb (is, am, are, seem, become) joins the subject to a word that describes or renames it. *She is a doctor.*

### 3. S + V + O + O

Two Objects

> ➕ **Added:** *He gave me a book.* (me = indirect object, book = direct object)

### 4. Helping V + S + V

Questions flip the order

> ➕ **Added:** *Are you coming?* / *Did she call?*

### 5. You + V + O

Commands hide the subject

> ➕ **Added:** *Close the door.* means *(You) close the door.*

### O + V + by S

Passive form

> ➕ **Added:** the full pattern is O + be verb + past participle + by S. *The code was written by Ravi.*

## Types of Sentence by Structure

> ➕ **Added:** **IC** = Independent Clause (makes complete sense alone). **DC** = Dependent Clause (cannot stand alone).

### 1. Simple

IC

> ➕ **Added:** *I study Java.*

### 2. Compound

IC + and / but / or / so + IC

> ➕ **Added:** *I studied hard, so I passed.*

### 3. Complex

DC + IC

> ➕ **Added:** *When the class ended, we went home.*

### 4. Compound-Complex

DC + IC + and + IC

> ➕ **Added:** *When the class ended, I went home and my friend went to the library.*

## Sentences by Purpose

### 1. Assertive

- Ends with `.`

> ➕ **Added:** states a fact or opinion. *Chennai is a city.*

### 2. Interrogative

- Ends with `?`

> ➕ **Added:** asks a question. *Where do you live?*

### 3. Imperative – order

- Ends with `.` & `!`

> ➕ **Added:** gives an order, request or advice. *Please sit down.* / *Stop!*

### 4. Exclamatory

- Ends with `!`

> ➕ **Added:** expresses strong feeling. *What a beautiful day!*
