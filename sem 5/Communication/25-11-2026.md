# Communication – Day 2

> Blocks marked **➕ Added** are not from the class notes.

## Professional Self-Introduction

1. Greetings
2. Name
3. Educational Credentials
4. Internship / Projects
5. Technical / Soft Skills
6. Certifications
7. Area of Interest

> ➕ **Added: what to say for each point**
>
> | Point | What to say | Sample line |
> |---|---|---|
> | Greetings | Greet according to the time | Good morning, everyone. |
> | Name | Your full name | My name is [name]. |
> | Educational Credentials | Degree, year, college | I am a [year] year [degree] student at [college]. |
> | Internship / Projects | One or two, with your role | I worked on [project], where I [your role]. |
> | Technical / Soft Skills | A few you can prove | My technical skills are [skills], and I am good at [soft skill]. |
> | Certifications | Relevant ones | I have completed a certification in [course]. |
> | Area of Interest | What you want to work in | I am interested in [area]. |
>
> **Closing:** Thank you for the opportunity.
>
> **Tips:** keep it to about 1 minute, follow the order above, speak slowly and keep eye contact.
